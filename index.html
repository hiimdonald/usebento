How it works

three taps · zero filing
STEP 01

Save it from any app.

Tap Share in Safari, Photos, Messages or your favorite social app, pick Bento, and choose a box. Or snap a photo, record a voice note, or type a quick note right in Bento.

STEP 02

It lands in the right box.

Pick a box, or let a smart filter file it for you. Bento recognizes what you saved: a recipe gets its ingredients and steps, a flight confirmation joins your trip, a dropped pin opens in Maps.

STEP 03

Find it by what's inside.

Search reads the words in your screenshots, finds photos by what’s in them, like “receipt” or “dog,” and matches by meaning, not just exact words. It all runs on your device.

03

Boxes that do something

new in v2

Some boxes do more than hold things.

Any box keeps whatever you drop in. Recipe, trip and home boxes add the tools for what’s inside, so a saved reel becomes dinner, a pile of confirmations becomes a trip, and a photo of a sticker becomes a warranty record.

Recipe boxes

From saved reel to dinner.

  • Bento reads the ingredients and steps from a link, a video caption or a photo.
  • Scale any recipe and switch between US and Metric.
  • Cook Mode goes one step at a time, with one-tap timers and Read Aloud. Turn on hands-free and say “next.”
  • Plan the week and get one grocery list, sorted by aisle and ready for Reminders.
Trip boxes

Your whole trip, in one box.

  • Plan by day on a map, with walking and travel times between stops.
  • Flight and hotel details come from your PDFs and screenshots.
  • Next Up keeps today’s flight, check-in or plan at the top.
  • A countdown and the weather for where you’re headed.
Home boxes

Know what your home needs next.

  • Model and serial numbers, manuals and warranties, room by room.
  • Read Label fills in the details from a photo of the sticker.
  • Upkeep reminders tell you when a filter or a check is due.
  • Coming Up shows what’s next across the house.

04  ·  what we don't take

Your library stays on your device.

No tracking, no cloud uploads by default, no sign-up. Text recognition, photo search and the search index all run on your iPhone or iPad. iCloud sync and shared boxes use your own iCloud, and we never see it. Lock a box with Face ID, or hide it completely.

↳ every tag, note and plan
↳ the entire search index
# what leaves your device
↳ nothing, unless you turn on iCloud sync or share a box
# and then it goes to your iCloud, not us
05

Questions

asked & answered
What are recipe, trip and home boxes?

They’re boxes with tools for what’s inside. A recipe box reads ingredients and steps and adds Cook Mode, a weekly plan and one grocery list. A trip box lays each day out on a map and pulls flight and hotel details from your PDFs and screenshots. A home box keeps model numbers, warranties and upkeep reminders. Any box can still hold anything you save.

Do I need an account to use Bento?

No. Install and start saving immediately: no email, no sign-in, no profile. If you want to sync across devices, Bento uses iCloud through your existing Apple ID. Nothing routes through our servers.

How can Bento search inside my screenshots?

Bento uses Apple's on-device text recognition (OCR) to read and index the words inside your images, all on your device, never uploaded. Once scanned, you can search any screenshot by the text it contains, just like a document. Bento also finds photos by what’s in them and matches searches by meaning.

What can I add to Bento?

Anything that shows up in the iOS share sheet: recipes, links, screenshots, photos, voice memos, PDFs, articles, places, contacts, Instagram posts, and more. Bento auto-detects the type — it knows what it is.

What's the difference between Lock and Hide?

Lock means a box is visible but gated by Face ID. Hide means the items don't appear in your inbox, search, or widgets at all. They live in a private vault you reveal with a pull-down gesture (or only from Settings, your choice).

Is Bento paid?

Every feature is free to use, including recipe, trip and home boxes. The free plan holds up to 200 saves, 20 boxes and 1 shared box, with iCloud sync and no account. Bento Pro removes those limits, as an annual subscription ($4.99/year, free trial included) or a one-time Founding Member purchase ($19.99). Both include every platform Bento ships on. The lifetime price will increase as features ship.

What devices does Bento support?

iPhone and iPad running iOS or iPadOS 26 or later. Mac is on the roadmap. Android is not currently planned.

Does Bento use Apple Intelligence?

Yes, where it's available. On iPhone 16 and later with Apple Intelligence enabled in Settings, Bento uses it to improve voice transcription and search. On older devices, Bento falls back to Apple's standard on-device tools, which still work well.
